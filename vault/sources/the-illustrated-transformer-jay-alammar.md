# The Illustrated Transformer (Jay Alammar)

## Takeaways
- Step-by-step vector and matrix walk-through of self-attention (query, key, value; score; scale; softmax; weighted sum).
- Explains multi-head attention, positional encoding, residual connections with layer norm, the decoder side, and the final linear and softmax layer.
- The post itself notes (2025 update) that newer models have since moved to variants such as Multi-Query Attention and RoPE.
