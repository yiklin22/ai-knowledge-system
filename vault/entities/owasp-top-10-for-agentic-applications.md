# OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications

## Who/what
- A document from the OWASP GenAI Security Project's Agentic Security Initiative, led by John Sotiropoulos (chair), Keren Katz and Ron F. Del Rosario, dated December 2025, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. [fact] (document p.1-2 and p.7)
- It maps each risk to the group's Agentic AI Threats and Mitigations taxonomy and to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications; each entry has description, examples, scenarios and mitigations. [fact] (document p.6-7)

## Why it matters
- It provides shared names (ASI01 to ASI10) and the "least agency" framing for discussing agent security. [fact] (document)
- Content is summarised on the concept page; only parts of it were read. [inference]
