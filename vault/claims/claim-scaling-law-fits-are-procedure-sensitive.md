# Scaling-law fits are sensitive to seemingly trivial procedural choices

## Evidence for
- Weng lists parameter counting, precision rounding and loss summing or averaging as choices that change predictions when extrapolated by orders of magnitude. [fact] (Weng 2026)
- The Kaplan/Chinchilla disagreement and Besiroglu et al.'s re-fit of Chinchilla Method 3 (optimizer termination, rounding to two digits) are the worked examples. [fact] (as reported by Weng 2026)

## Evidence against
- None recorded. [inference]

## Verdict reasoning
Supported by concrete examples in a single secondary survey; primary papers not yet ingested. [inference]
