# Why You Should Write Weekly 15-5s

## Takeaways
- [fact] Format: 15 minutes to write, 5 to read, learned from Gergely Orosz and Will Larson (who call it a 5-15).
- [fact] Benefit 1: scalable visibility for manager, team and subscribed stakeholders, freeing 1-on-1s for strategic issues.
- [fact] Benefit 2: a log of work, outcomes and blockers that reveals what slows the team over time.
