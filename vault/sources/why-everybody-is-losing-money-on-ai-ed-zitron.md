# Why Everybody Is Losing Money On AI (Ed Zitron)

## Takeaways
- Thesis: every generative AI company except training-data vendors is losing money and there is no sign margins will improve; "costs will come down" is dismissed as wrong.
- Cited figures (attributed by the author to The Information, WSJ and others, not checked here): OpenAI lost $5 billion and Anthropic $5.3 billion in 2024; Perplexity spent 164% of 2024 revenue on cloud and model providers; Notion's AI costs ate about 10% of its profit margin.
- Argument that inference cost per task is rising because reasoning models burn more tokens, and that Cursor sends all its revenue to Anthropic.
- Strongly worded, forecast-heavy ("I expect OpenAI to burn at least $15 billion") and ends at a paywall; the premium part with the supporting detail was not ingested.
- Dated September 2025: finances and prices have moved since. Compare Willison's September 2026 account of agent spending and product-market fit.
