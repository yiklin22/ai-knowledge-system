# OpenClaw

## Who/what
- Willison says it began as an obscure repository called "Warelay" in November 2025 and was renamed several times (CLAWDIS, CLAWDBOT, Moltbot) before OpenClaw; he reports over 100,000 commits by late September 2026 and calls it the most vibe-coded software in existence. [fact] (Willison 2026, slides 005 and 015-016; secondary)
- It kicked off a category he calls "Claws", with variants such as NanoClaw, IronClaw and PicoClaw; he reports Mac Mini shortages and install parties in China. [fact] (Willison 2026, slides 017-018 and 030)
- Su names OpenClaw and Hermes as open-source agents you can run locally for privacy, at the cost of harder setup and weaker local models. [fact] (Su video, 13:32; presenter's view)

## Why it matters
- It is the clearest example in these sources of non-expert demand for personal agents, and of the security question that comes with giving one broad access. [inference]
- Commit counts and market claims are from commentators, not the project itself. [inference]
