# Why do LLM scaling laws take the form of a power law?

## Why I'm asking
Weng lists two hypotheses: language modeling as regression on a low-dimensional data manifold (Sharma and Kaplan 2020), and skills learned in discrete chunks with power-law frequency (Michaud et al. 2023, Brill 2024). Weng says there are more explanations via spectral tails, kernel eigenvalues, language statistics and phase transitions.

## What would answer it
A theory that predicts the exponents, not just the shape. Relevant sources: the primary papers named above, none yet in `raw/`. See also [[do-scaling-laws-keep-holding-as-models-and-data-grow]].
