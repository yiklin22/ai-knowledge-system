# Positron AI funding and Asimov roadmap (X-AI blog)

## Takeaways
- Funding: announced 10 September 2026, nominal $875 million at a $5 billion valuation, structured as a $375 million Series C plus up to $500 million Series C-1; the second part is conditional.
- Roadmap: Asimov is planned to tape out on TSMC N3P at the end of 2026, with production planned for the second half of 2027; the article stresses that tape-out is not mass production, shipping or delivery.
- Product states: Atlas is the first generation, with 50+ racks reportedly deployed on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure; Titan is "Coming in 2027" and powered by 4 or 8 Asimov chips; the OCI deployment belongs to Atlas, not Titan.
- Asimov uses LPDDR5X rather than HBM per the product page; the performance numbers are from cycle-accurate simulation, not measured silicon.
- Secondary summary of company press material in Traditional Chinese; written cautiously, but all facts trace to Positron and the press release, which were not ingested.
