# Generative AI providers are unprofitable and costs are not falling

Statement (as of September 2025): no generative AI model provider, and no company building its core product on them, is profitable, and the cost of inference is rising.

## Evidence for
- Zitron cites reported losses (OpenAI $5 billion and Anthropic $5.3 billion in 2024), Perplexity spending 164% of 2024 revenue on cloud and model providers and Notion's AI costs taking about 10% of margin; he attributes these to The Information and WSJ and they were not checked here. [fact] (Zitron, opinion; figures second-hand)
- He argues reasoning models burn more tokens, so cost per task is rising. [fact] (Zitron)
- Willison (September 2026) says agents made spending large, "tokenmaxxing" rose then fell as companies capped use, and you can spend $1,000 a day on real work. [fact] (Willison 2026, slide 028; secondary)

## Evidence against
- Zitron himself reports that OpenAI claims to be profitable on inference and has leaked a 48% gross margin for 2025, and he dismisses these as accounting choices. [fact] (Zitron)
- Willison says AI appears to have hit product-market fit in 2026 mainly through coding agents and links this to Anthropic's valuation; that is demand, not profit. [fact] (Willison 2026, slide 028)
- Both sources predate or sit outside audited accounts; neither gives current margin data. [inference]

## Verdict reasoning
- Contested and time-bound: the unprofitability claim rests on one polemical, partly paywalled source from over a year ago, while later evidence shows high spend and demand without settling margins. Re-check with filings before relying on it. [inference]
- Volatile: figures and prices change quickly; last verified against the ingested pages on 2026-11-08. [fact]
