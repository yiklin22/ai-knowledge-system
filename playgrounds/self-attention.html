Self-attention playground

Two experiments. The first uses hand-chosen toy vectors, not learned ones, to show who a word attends to. The second shows why attention divides by √dk.

Predict first. In “The animal didn't cross the street because it was too tired”, which word will the query “it” attend to most?

1. Who does this word attend to?

Click any word to make it the query. Each cell shows its attention weight (the row sums to 100%).

2. Why divide by √dk?

Random queries and keys, each component with mean 0 and variance 1 (as in the paper's footnote). Averages over 300 trials of 8 keys.
Without scaling: std of scores · strongest weight
With 1/√dk: std of scores · strongest weight
A near-100% strongest weight means softmax is saturated: tiny gradients, so learning stalls. Slide dk up and watch the red bar.
